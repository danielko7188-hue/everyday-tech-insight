## Content and evidence

- [ ] Every published article has been read in full by the owner.
- [ ] Every article passes the five-part Business Technology Fit Test in substance, not only schema shape.
- [ ] Material claims are accurate and remain inside the cited evidence.
- [ ] Current or time-sensitive information has been rechecked directly against official sources.
- [ ] No personal experience, observed result, test, customer use, credential, or expertise was fabricated.
- [ ] Sources are appropriate, primary where required, and fairly summarized.
- [ ] Product-category comparisons are fair and do not turn vendor definitions into performance claims.
- [ ] Pricing and plan claims have been rechecked; the launch portfolio intentionally makes no live product-price claim.
- [ ] Article publication and review dates are genuine; `dateModified` remains absent until a later substantive change.
- [ ] Every limitation, legal boundary, and specialist-escalation statement is appropriate.
- [ ] The content is original editorial synthesis and does not copy excessive source language.
- [ ] Related-reading links are genuinely useful and resolve to published articles.

## Images, rights, and attribution

- [ ] Every public image, icon, illustration, and other media item is owned, licensed, public domain, or otherwise authorized.

- [ ] No third-party screenshot or product logo is added without rights and context review.
