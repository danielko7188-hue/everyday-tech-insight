## Project choices are not universal approval requirements

The project's 18 owner gates remain useful operating records. They should not all be represented as Google eligibility rules. The reviewed official guidance does not set universal article-count, word-count, traffic, domain-age, named-author, portrait, Terms-page, or Lighthouse-score thresholds. The repository's initial 15-guide history, minimum body length, source-count rules, and author schema are local quality controls.

A custom domain may improve branding, but the current hostname is not inherently disqualified: Google's site-management guidance permits platform subdomains on the public suffix list, and the authoritative list consulted on September 9 contains `vercel.app`. This is an inference about the hostname category, not confirmation that Google has accepted this specific site.

Google describes `ads.txt` as recommended rather than mandatory. Its current absence while advertising is off is not itself a universal application blocker. Any future seller line must come from the correct account, not a sample identifier.

## Remaining evidence before an approval-level claim

1. Actual human editorial review or curation of the AI-assisted guides, with corrections and an identified acceptance record. Specialist review is needed where the consequence or claim exceeds the reviewer's competence; a universal requirement to hire a lawyer for every guide was not found.
2. Verified owner/account eligibility, the exact submitted site, its ownership-verification method, and actual AdSense site and Policy Center status. These are not available from the repository.
3. Confirmation of the real operator facts and public disclosures needed for the actual operation, including an appropriate contact route and asset rights. Do not infer a person's legal identity or a copyright license from a username or a successful build.
4. Before advertising: accurate commercial/privacy disclosures, approved account values, appropriate route placement, and applicable consent configuration. Keep error, empty, and navigation-only screens outside advertising inventory.
5. Production verification of the precise release submitted for review. Google alone makes the approval decision.

The separate Blogger theme remains governed by its own Preview-only status. This static-site review does not establish Blogger compatibility or authorize Blogger publication.
