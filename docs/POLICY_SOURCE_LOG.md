## Conflicts and unresolved items

- No reviewed official source conflicted with the requirement to publish useful original content, keep claims within evidence, and avoid approval guarantees.
- NIST explicitly states that the AI RMF 1.0 is being revised. The articles therefore identify the framework version and avoid claiming permanence.
- Several government sources are intentionally broader or older than the small-business implementation. Every affected article discloses the source context and labels the exact method as editorial adaptation.
- The direct reachability snapshot is not continuous monitoring. Links and source meaning must be rechecked during material updates and immediately before any high-stakes use.
- Human review remains required for owner identity, legal/privacy duties, product-specific claims, pricing, accessibility, content accuracy, and any future AdSense or consent configuration.
