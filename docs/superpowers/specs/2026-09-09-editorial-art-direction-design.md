# Editorial Art Direction

## Decision and scope

The owner rejected the first Apple-inspired pass as insufficiently high-end. This contract supersedes its appearance requirements. Keep Apple-like restraint, but make actual guidance the visual subject. Existing standing instruction is autonomous execution and GitHub before Vercel; no new account, monetization or Blogger actions.

## Observed problem

Fresh native captures at 390/1440 show the first home guide at y967/y1104; the category taxonomy is 72px while its first guide is 24.4px and starts at y804. The sampled mobile article's prose begins at y1844; its fit summary occupies 666px. These are measured positions, not design scores. ## Alternatives

1. Continue CSS-only refinement: low risk, but leaves the document-template composition intact. Rejected.
2. Copy Apple's product-launch page: strong spectacle, wrong emphasis for a small practical publication. Rejected.
3. Story-led editorial front page, purpose-made cover imagery, restrained reading architecture. Selected.

## Design

- White publication frame, soft-gray feature surface, charcoal type and blue actions. No purple, circuits, blobs, fake device UI, advertising or ornamental animation.
- Self-hosted Instrument Sans variable (OFL-1.1), 400-700, for display/UI/body. Licensed Source Sans remains for existing informative diagrams and deterministic social previews. No copied Apple fonts or assets.
- Home: brief publication introduction, not a separate marketing hero. Preserve existing H1, introduction, two destinations and nine distinct curated guide destinations. Remove the unrelated glass-object hero. One prominent lead story with a large subject-specific cover and a 40-48px desktop title; two secondary stories below. First actual guide title must be visible within 780px at 390 and 1440.
- Category: compact title plus description; remove redundant generic eyebrow and purpose paragraph. All three current guides remain in their existing order. The first is a split image/text feature; the other two use balanced editorial rows. No duplicate guide links or repeated category labels in same-category card metadata. Existing empty/editorial/archive membership logic stays intact.
- Covers: five coordinated conceptual still lifes for automation, software, account security, operations and strategy. Generated with the built-in image tool, no logos or factual/product-testing implications. Decorative `alt=""`, adjacent visible `AI-generated editorial illustration` credit, explicit dimensions and responsive delivery. No text embedded in pictures. Preserve originals and prompt/provenance record. Do not replace informative diagrams inside guides with decoration.
- Article: title, summary, byline/date and understated evidence links remain visible. Fit details become a clearly labeled native disclosure closed initially. Existing guide diagram stays visible, placed inside the reading column after a brief visible takeaway/fit entry; no cropped labels. Target reduced opening burden without removing sources, limitations, headings or schema. Native TOC anchors and sticky behavior retained.
- Supporting pages inherit typography and consistent frame, not decorative new illustrations everywhere. Existing menus, downloads, content lifecycle, URL taxonomy, trust destinations and security settings unchanged.
- Desktop container 1120-1200px; mobile gutters 20px (16px at320). Feature uses one intentional 20px radius; normal rows, forms and reading content stay flat. Body at least16px, reading prose18px. Mobile layout is re-composed, not a mini desktop. No scroll-jacking, required JS, hover-only controls or delayed content reveal.

## Acceptance

Native visual comparison at320/390/768/1024/1440 plus full eight-width production audit. First-guide visibility, no overflow, real image loads, native disclosure keyboard behavior, accessible names, focus, reduced motion, no-JS reading and unchanged content membership are explicit regressions. Preserve functional validators. Appearance assertions and screenshots may change only when directly superseded by this contract; log each such change. Never relax screenshot tolerance, CI or content/security policy to force green. Full `npm run qa`, independent spec and code review, protected GitHub merge, exact-SHA Vercel smoke and final CI are required before release.
