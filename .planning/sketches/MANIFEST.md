# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction

Purple Signal Editorial balances a serious independent publication with restrained modern-technology energy. Dark plum surfaces establish identity and story openings; light lavender and paper surfaces protect long-form reading. Violet supplies structure, magenta is a limited signal, and typography—not decoration—carries most hierarchy.

## Reference Points

- WEKA: high-contrast technical energy and luminous restraint only; no copied mark, palette, gradient, composition, or assets.
- Semafor and IEEE Spectrum: scannable story hierarchy and practical information density.
- The New Yorker and WIRED: confident editorial typography and comfortable reading surfaces.
- Current Everyday Tech Insight: truthful static architecture, five-topic taxonomy, unique explanatory visuals, and evidence-first copy.
