# Sketch 001: Publication Shell

## Design Question

How should Purple Signal Editorial balance dark identity surfaces, bright signal accents, and light editorial reading areas?

## How to View

Open `.planning/sketches/001-publication-shell/index.html`.

## Variants

- **A: Balanced Signal — selected** — Dark two-row masthead and opening band, followed quickly by paper editorial modules.
- **B: Night Edition** — A more immersive dark homepage with greater technological energy and higher contextual-color cost.
- **C: Quiet Violet** — A mostly light magazine treatment with excellent calm but less distinctive identity.

## What to Look For

Compare brand recognition, first-story visibility, dark/light pacing, headline authority, and whether the page feels like a publication rather than enterprise software.

## Decision

Variant A is selected because it best matches the requested 70% editorial / 30% modern-technology balance while minimizing dark-surface accessibility risk.
