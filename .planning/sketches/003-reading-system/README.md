# Sketch 003: Reading System

## Design Question

How should the article hero, at-a-glance information, table of contents, body, evidence, and Toolkit actions work together without duplicating content or overwhelming the reader?

## How to View

Open `.planning/sketches/003-reading-system/index.html`.

## Variants

- **A: Evidence-first Reading — selected** — Dark 7/5 hero, light four-part context, one TOC, restrained evidence, and a separate Toolkit action.
- **B: Immersive Feature** — Larger visual drama, but it delays the practical method and feels more magazine-like than useful.
- **C: Quiet Reading** — Excellent long-form calm, but does not carry enough Purple Signal identity into article entry.

## What to Look For

Compare time-to-content, evidence visibility, mobile stacking, body comfort, and the distance between Toolkit explanations and downloads.

## Decision

Variant A is selected because it makes the practical output and evidence boundary explicit while keeping the body light, readable, and ad-free.
